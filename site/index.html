Good for this, right now

Hand-written, with prices looked up fresh on every build. A pick whose price has drifted is flagged rather than left to quietly mislead.

Bulk and mechanical work

Log triage, batch classification, mass edits — anything you run thousands of times

Claude Haiku 4.5  $1 / $5 per MTok

Cheapest thing Anthropic sells that is still current-generation. At this volume the model choice dominates the bill, and the work rarely needs more.

The cheapest current-generation model anywhere

When you want frontier-family quality at old-mini prices

GPT-5.6 Luna (≤ 200K)  $0.2 / $1.2 per MTok

An outlier on price — roughly a tenth of Sonnet 5 on input after a 80% cut in July that has held. Worth reaching for on anything high-volume before assuming you need something dearer.

Everyday coding and general work

The default that is hard to argue with

Claude Sonnet 5  $2 / $10 per MTok

Undercuts its whole class since the introductory price was made permanent on 1 September 2026 — the scheduled rise to $3/$15 was cancelled, so this is a third cheaper than Sonnet 4.6 for newer work.

The hardest reasoning

When a wrong answer costs more than the tokens

Claude Opus 5  $5 / $25 per MTok

Half the price of the old Opus 4.1 at $15/$75. Remember the tokenizer caveat though: Claude 4.7 and later emit about 30% more tokens for the same text, so per-token prices understate the gap against Sonnet 4.6 and earlier.

Long context you re-read many times

Big documents or codebases held across many calls

Claude Fable 5.1  $10 / $50 per MTok

Expensive per token, but cache reads are 0.025x base input rather than the usual 0.1x — $0.25/MTok against a $10 list price. If the same context is read repeatedly, that quarter-price cache changes the arithmetic entirely. The 1M context window is billed at standard rates on 4.6 and later.

A legacy annual Copilot plan

Only if you stayed on premium-request billing after 1 June 2026

Claude Haiku 4.5

The multiplier spread is brutal — 0.33x here against 27x for Opus 4.8 and 57x for GPT-5.5. Copilot code review alone costs 13x per review. On these plans the model picker matters more than anything else you can change.
